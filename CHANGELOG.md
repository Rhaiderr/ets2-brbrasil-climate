Regional summer temperatures for BR Brasil 6.1 / ETS2 1.62. Includes historical climate references, variable native weather states and gradual regional interpolation. Activate above the map packages.

Description separators are corrected. Climate and map entries are identical to the tested 1.0.1 package. Mountainous sectors can retain height-related inaccuracies; future map versions need review. Weather_3.9 compatibility is unverified.
